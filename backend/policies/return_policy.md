# Return Policy — ShopEasy Customer Support

**Version:** 1.0 (Demo Policy — for local evaluation only)
**Effective:** 2024-01-01

---

## 1. General Return Policy

Customers may return eligible items within the specified return window from the date of delivery.
Items must be in original, unused condition with all packaging and accessories.

---

## 2. Category-Specific Return Windows

| Category     | Return Window | Conditions                                   |
|-------------|--------------|----------------------------------------------|
| Electronics  | 30 days      | Must be unused; original packaging required  |
| Clothing     | 15 days      | Unworn, tags attached                        |
| Food         | 7 days       | Only if damaged or incorrect item            |
| Books        | 15 days      | Undamaged, no highlights or writing          |
| Home & Kitchen | 15 days   | Unused, original packaging                  |
| Other        | 15 days      | Assessed case-by-case                        |

---

## 3. Non-Returnable Items

The following items are **not eligible for return or refund**:
- Perishable goods past 7 days after delivery
- Digital downloads and software licenses
- Personalized or custom-made items
- Hazardous materials
- Items marked as "Final Sale"

---

## 4. Refund Process

1. Customer contacts support via chat or ticket.
2. AI agent retrieves order details and applicable policy.
3. Backend validates: ownership, delivery date, return window, amount.
4. Refund requests **up to ₹5,000** may be processed automatically if eligible.
5. Refund requests **above ₹5,000** require human review by support staff.
6. Approved refunds are simulated (no real payment processing).
7. Processing time: 5-7 business days after approval.

---

## 5. Refund Eligibility Criteria

A refund is eligible if:
- The order has been delivered
- The return window has not expired
- The item is in returnable condition
- No previous refund has been approved for the same order
- The requested amount does not exceed the order total

---

## 6. Escalation Policy

Escalation to human review is triggered when:
- Refund amount exceeds ₹5,000
- Customer reports damaged/wrong items with photo evidence requested
- Dispute or complaint about AI response
- Order involves special circumstances

Staff may **approve**, **reject**, or **request more information**.

---

## 7. Electronics Return Policy (Detailed)

Electronics have a **30-day return window** from delivery date.
- Must be powered on less than 5 times for unused status
- All accessories, cables, manuals must be included
- Original box and packaging required
- Software installed or accounts created may void eligibility
- Manufacturing defects covered regardless of window

---

## 8. Clothing Return Policy (Detailed)

Clothing has a **15-day return window** from delivery date.
- Items must be unworn with original tags attached
- Washed items are non-returnable
- Undergarments and swimwear are non-returnable for hygiene reasons
- Sale items may have reduced return window (check item listing)

---

## 9. Food and Perishables Policy

Food items have a **7-day return window** from delivery.
- Only eligible if item is damaged, spoiled on arrival, or wrong item sent
- Customer must provide photo evidence
- Perishable items past 7 days are non-returnable

---

## 10. Contact and Support

For refund or return queries:
- Use the AI support chat in your account
- Raise a ticket in the customer portal
- Support staff available Monday–Friday, 9 AM–6 PM IST
